## About the Game

**Sector Null** is a single-player, first-person psychological survival horror game set inside a mysterious underground research facility.

You wake up as a prisoner after a failed experiment. The facility is collapsing, the power is unstable, and something is hunting through the sectors. To escape, you must explore locked areas, manage limited resources, solve environmental puzzles, and uncover the truth through scattered logs, terminals, and hidden evidence.

This game focuses on atmosphere, tension, exploration, and survival rather than constant combat.

---

## Core Gameplay

- First-person horror exploration
- Survival resource management
- Batteries, health items, keycards, and puzzle items
- Locked sectors and access-based progression
- Environmental puzzles involving power, generators, and doors
- Enemy encounters and stealth-based tension
- Storytelling through logs, terminals, and visual clues
- Save/load progression system
